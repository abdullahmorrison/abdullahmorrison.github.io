Overlays for Twitch

Three OBS browser sources. Paste a link in and they work — no download, no login, no account to connect.

Tomato'd

Let your chat throw tomatoes at you. A mod types !tomato, and for the next thirty seconds anyone saying the magic word hurls one at the screen, where it splatters.

Stream End Credits

Thank everyone who showed up, as film credits. Watches chat all stream, then rolls your subs, gifters, raiders and cheerers when you switch to your ending scene.

Stream Breaking News

A mod types !breaking and whatever they wrote crawls along the bottom of your stream in a proper cable-news lower third. The headline stays deadpan. The joke is the ticker.
